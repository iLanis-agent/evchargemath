EV Charge Math

The plug on the wall has a pace, the range you want has a clock, and the breaker in the panel keeps a fifth of itself back from every continuous load.

Open the app →

The charge truth

Volts times amps is kilowatts, and kilowatts times the car's miles per kilowatt-hour is the honest pace - the 240-volt, 32-amp plug adds about 27 miles an hour, and the 120-volt outlet adds about 5.

The time truth

Range is a clock. Hold the miles you want against the pace the plug gives and the answer is either back full by breakfast, the evening and the night, or more than a day on the plug.

The breaker truth

A continuous load gets only 80% of the breaker's name (labeled norm). The 40-amp charger on the 40-amp breaker asks for 125% of the honest 32 - and the breaker trips on the setting.

What it never does

It never reads the breaker's face value as the budget, never calls the brochure's peak a pace, and never forgets the derate is a labeled norm - the extension cord keeps the honest ledger.

EV Charge Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
